# Interactive site spec

Page = `index.html`, long and chaptered, dark "night lab" identity (tokens in `assets/css/base.css`; use the CSS variables, never new literal colors except via those tokens). Each chapter is one file `assets/js/sections/<id>.js` that calls:

```js
EBT.section({ id: '<id>', nav: 'Short nav label', kicker: 'Lab', title: 'Chapter title', lede: 'One or two sentences (HTML ok).', mount(el, lib) { ... } });
```

`lib` = `EBT.lib` = all video-engine helpers (`EBTV.U`: text, axes, plot, heatmap, contours, surface, cmap, rng, ease...) + DOM helpers in `assets/js/lib.js` (`lib.h`, `lib.canvas`, `lib.slider`, `lib.segmented`, `lib.badge`, `lib.callout`, `lib.loop`, `lib.whenVisible`, `lib.fmt`, `lib.data(name)`). The toy 2D model helper `assets/js/toy2d_model.js` (written by the lead) exposes `EBT.toy2d` (see that file). Optional CSS: `assets/css/sections/<id>.css`, every selector prefixed with `#<id>` or `.<id>-`.

Rules:
- Works from `file://`: no fetch, no modules, no CDN, no network. Data comes from `window.EBT_DATA` (`lib.data('toy2d')`, etc.), images by relative path (`media/...`).
- Each lab = explanation (short, plain) + instrument (canvas/SVG) + controls (sliders, segmented buttons, step/play/reset) + live readouts. Start in a meaningful state (not empty), and animate only when visible or when the user presses Play (`lib.whenVisible`, `lib.loop`). Respect `lib.reducedMotion`.
- Every visual carries a provenance badge: `lib.badge('paper','Fig 4a')`, `lib.badge('toy')`, `lib.badge('ext')`.
- Numbers from the paper must match `notes/paper_facts.md`. Toy results are toy results.
- Responsive: works at 390 px wide (stack controls above instrument; canvases scale by CSS width). No horizontal page scroll. Text ≥ 14 px. Keyboard-usable controls with labels. Canvases get `aria-label`.
- Performance: keep each frame < 16 ms on a laptop; precompute grids; cap pixel work (e.g. 96×96 grids upscaled).
- Test: `python3 src/bundle_data.py` (after data changes), then `node src/shot_section.js <id>` and LOOK at `video/frames/site_<id>_desktop.png` and `_phone.png`; fix overflow and console errors. Use `ACTIONS='...js...' node src/shot_section.js <id> after` to screenshot after interactions (e.g. clicking Play/Step) and confirm the instrument changes.

## Chapters

- `watch` (lead): the 10-minute video with chapter buttons + link to the frame-accurate canvas player.
- `families` — "Four ways to predict the next token". Interactive side-by-side of AR Transformer, RNN, Diffusion Transformer, EBT generating the next token for a chosen prompt (easy: "The cat sat on the" vs hard: "The quick brown"). Controls: prompt (easy/hard), "compute budget" slider (forward passes allowed 1–16), Step / Play / Reset. Each lane animates its mechanism and shows: passes used, can it use more passes?, does it output a score?, uncertainty readout. EBT lane uses a small analytic or toy energy so its energy readout really decreases and converges slower on the hard prompt; label honestly (illustration). Below: Table 1 (facets) as an interactive table where hovering a cell explains why (with page refs). Closing callout on diffusion = learned gradient of an energy (Sec 6.4).
- `landscape` — "Energy landscape lab" (core). Live toy 2D EBT from `toy2d.json` via `EBT.toy2d`. Instrument: heatmap + contours over ŷ (left, clickable: click to place ŷ0) and a rotatable 3D surface (right, drag to rotate) of the same landscape. Controls: context picker (the toy's contexts, with noise level shown), step size α, steps N, Langevin noise σ, candidates M (Best-of-N), Step / Run / Reset, "show truth" toggle. Readouts: energy per step plot for every candidate, chosen candidate (min energy), distance to truth. A second panel: "Loss landscape vs energy landscape" toggle showing `param_landscape` (training loss over θ along two random directions) next to the energy landscape, with a clear explanation of the difference.
- `thinking` — "Text thinking lab". From `text.json`: pick an example context; scrub thinking steps 0..N: top-8 next-character probability bars animate, energy per step plot, entropy readout; easy-vs-hard energy heatmap over a sentence (characters × steps) like paper Fig 8, with the paper's Fig 8 crop alongside (badge paper); thinking curve (val loss vs steps and vs BoN M) for the toy EBT vs the flat baseline (badge toy), next to digitized Fig 6a/6b/7 (badge paper, approx). If `text.json` has weights, run the model live on user-typed context.
- `training` — "Training lab". (1) Algorithm 1 stepper: an animated unrolled graph ŷ0→ŷ1→…→ŷN→loss with forward and backward passes, user chooses N (1–6) and sees how the gradient to θ is assembled from every step (explain Hessian-vector products simply). (2) A 1D toy you can train by clicking "train step": E_θ(ŷ) = a small parametric function (e.g. θ0·(ŷ−θ1)² + θ2·sin(ŷ)), target y; shows unrolled optimization, loss, dL/dθ and the landscape reshaping each update (exact gradients via forward-mode or finite differences, label ext/illustration). (3) Checkpoint scrubber over `toy2d.json` checkpoints: landscape morphing + training loss curve with a cursor (badge toy). (4) Regularizers: toggles for replay buffer, Langevin, random α, random N with short explanations and Table 2 ablation bars (badge paper).
- `images` — "Denoising lab". Real COCO samples (`samples.json`) with a noise slider expressed as σ (fraction of the linear β schedule, compute ᾱ live) and markers at 0.1 (train) and 0.2 (OOD); toy denoising EBT strips from `image.json`/`media/toy/` with a thinking-step scrubber and PSNR readout; PSNR-vs-forward-passes chart: toy curves (badge toy) and paper Fig 12 digitized (badge paper, approx.) + Table 4 values; the paper's Fig 10 crop. Include a "how PSNR is computed" mini explainer.
- `planning` — "Planning & MPC with energy" (badge ext; clearly beyond the paper). 2D point-robot world with start, goal, draggable circular obstacles. A plan = H future actions (velocities). Energy(plan) = goal term + obstacle penalty + smoothness/effort + dynamics consistency, all differentiable (write analytic gradients). Controls: horizon H, step size, planning iterations per control step, Langevin noise, candidates M (BoN), Play/Step/Reset, "re-plan every step (MPC)" toggle vs "open-loop". Visualize: all candidate plans as faint paths updating each iteration ("thinking"), the chosen min-energy plan bold, executed trajectory trail, energy-vs-iteration plot, counters for planning iterations and MPC steps. Text explains how this maps to EBT (plan = prediction ŷ, context = state + goal, energy = learned verifier in a real system; gradient descent on the plan = thinking; BoN = self-verification) and to JEPA world models (energy computed in a learned latent space).
- `scaling` — "Scaling laws explorer". From `scaling.json`: axis picker (data, batch, depth, params, FLOPs, width; video width, video params), log-log chart with digitized points (badge paper, approx.), fitted power-law lines, slope readouts and "scaling-rate gain"; an extrapolation slider (×1 to ×1000 of the largest point) that extends dashed fits with a clear warning; a "what does a slope mean" mini explainer; thinking-scaling charts (Fig 6b, Fig 7). Show the compute caveat (EBT step cost) from paper_facts.
- `data` — "Data and evaluation". Tabs: Pretraining text (RedPajama snippets as GPT-NeoX token chips with ids on hover, context/target pairs), Evaluation (GSM8K, SQuAD, BB Math QA, BB Dyck examples and an interactive "how perplexity is computed" widget: per-token probabilities → NLL → ppl), Images (COCO grid, σ=0.1/0.2 versions, patchification), Video (paper Fig 11 + explanation of SD-XL VAE latents), Splits & setups table (datasets, sizes, losses, metrics, baselines).
- `takeaways` — "What to take away". Why EBTs beat the baselines (each claim linked to evidence: figure/table + chapter link), Limitations (from paper_facts), Open questions for a JEPA / world-model / continual-learning researcher (e.g. EBT energy in JEPA latent space, using energy as a novelty signal, replay buffer vs continual learning, thinking under distribution shift), Glossary from `glossary.json` (searchable), and a "paper map" table of every figure/table with one-line meaning.
