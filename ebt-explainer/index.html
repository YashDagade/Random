Energy-Based Transformers learn to think by optimizing against their own verifier

A step-by-step, interactive reading of Energy-Based Transformers are Scalable Learners and Thinkers. Each section pairs a live figure with the text and equations behind it. Drag predictions across real learned energy landscapes, unroll training one gradient at a time, watch a text model think, and plan with an energy.
